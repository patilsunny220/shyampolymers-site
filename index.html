Acetate for eyewear

Granules that mould into frames people want to wear.

Our cellulose acetate compounds are made for injection moulding of spectacle fronts, temples and sunglass frames. Each batch is colour-checked against its master and dried, bagged and labelled with a batch number you can trace.

Frame makers use us instead of importing because we hold stock in Gujarat, match colours to their samples and send trial quantities free.

  • Phthalate-free plasticisers
  • Consistent melt flow from batch to batch
  • Polishes to a deep gloss after moulding
  • Packed in 25 kg moisture-barrier bags

Where our raw materials come from

We buy cellulose acetate flake and additives from established producers in Europe and the USA, and compound them in Gujarat. Every incoming lot is tested before it reaches the line.

Sustainability

A plastic that starts as a plant

Made from wood pulp and cotton

Cellulose acetate comes from plant cellulose, not crude oil. It is the traditional material of fine eyewear for that reason.

Tested to biodegrade

Our acetate compounds are tested for biodegradation under ISO 14855 industrial composting conditions. Reports available on request.

Sprues and runners taken back

Frame makers can return clean moulding scrap. We regrind it and put it back into use instead of sending it to landfill.

Company

A Vadodara family business, now in its second generation

Our founder came to Vadodara as a young man and started in the plastics trade. What began as a small trading business grew into compounding, and then into acetate for the eyewear industry.

Today the business is run by his son, with the same rule the founder started with: every bag that leaves the plant should match the last one.

  1. 1970sFounder settles in Vadodara and starts trading plastics.
  2. 1980sFirst compounding line. Acetate granules for combs and hair accessories.
  3. 1990sEyewear-grade acetate. Frame makers become our main customers.
  4. 2003Shyam Polymers registered. Nylon 6 and PBT added.
  5. 2010sHalol plant opens. Acetate capacity reaches 100 tonnes a year.
  6. TodayColour lab, phthalate-free range and the second generation at the helm.
