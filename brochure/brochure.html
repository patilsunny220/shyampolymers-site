Acetate for eyewear

Granules that mould into frames people want to wear

Our cellulose acetate compounds are made for injection moulding of spectacle fronts, temples and sunglass frames. Every batch is colour-checked against its master, dried, bagged and labelled with a traceable batch number. We hold stock in Gujarat, match colours to your sample and send trial quantities free.

  • Phthalate-free plasticisers
  • Consistent melt flow from batch to batch
  • Polishes to a deep gloss after moulding
  • 25 kg moisture-barrier bags

Printed and on-screen colours are a guide. Ask for physical chips and moulded plaques. Custom colours matched to your sample in 7 to 10 working days.

Company

A Vadodara family business, now in its second generation

Our founder came to Vadodara as a young man and started in the plastics trade. The business grew into compounding, and then into acetate for the eyewear industry. Today it is run by his son, with the rule the founder started with: every bag that leaves the plant should match the last one.
